Short description: Bushfires, floods and road crashes near you in Australia, on one map.

See what is happening near you. Emergencies Near Me shows bushfires, floods, road crashes, storms, hazmat incidents and ambulance calls published by Australian emergency services, on one map and in one list, within the radius you choose around your location or a place you pick.

Not a government app: Emergencies Near Me is an independent app. It does not represent any government, government agency or emergency service, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Incident information can be delayed or incomplete. In an emergency, call 000. Always follow the advice of official emergency services.

• Map of current incidents
• Nearby list, newest first, with distance and warning level or status; it checks for new incidents every minute while the app is open
• One radius for everything, or a radius per service: for example bushfires 100 km and road crashes 10 km
• Use your current location, or pick a fixed place
• Advice, Watch and Act and Emergency Warning levels, as issued by the agencies
• Victoria, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia and the ACT in one app
• Incident details: agency, status, resources where published, directions and a link to the official source

Push alerts are not available on Android yet; they are coming in a later update. Until then, open the app to see what is happening near you.

Sources
All incident information comes from the public feeds of these government emergency services. Check the official source for the latest information:
• Victoria: VicEmergency, https://emergency.vic.gov.au/respond/
• New South Wales: NSW Rural Fire Service, https://www.rfs.nsw.gov.au/fire-information/fires-near-me
• Queensland: Queensland Fire Department, https://www.fire.qld.gov.au/Current-Incidents
• South Australia: SA Country Fire Service, https://www.cfs.sa.gov.au/incidents/
• Australian Capital Territory: ACT Emergency Services Agency, https://esa.act.gov.au/
Every incident in the app names its source and links to it. Sources and licences: https://emergenciesnearme.online/sources
