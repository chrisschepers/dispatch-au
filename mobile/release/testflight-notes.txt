Dispatch Victoria — simpler navigation (0.1.2, build 3)

Only three tabs: Nearby, Map and Settings. A compact scrolling location/category header, quieter incident cards and a floating tab bar follow the 112 Meldingen layout. The map gets most of the screen. Choose your area in the location sheet and set its radius under Settings. No Pro promotions or separate Places tab.

Please test cold launch, switching all three tabs, category filters, incident details, Show on map, area selection and radius, and light/dark appearance. Pull down the Nearby list to refresh.

Official warnings remain visible across Victoria regardless of incident filters. The independent preview refreshes while open and needs an internet connection. Always follow VicEmergency; call 000 in an emergency. Background alerts, cloud sync and purchases are not available.
