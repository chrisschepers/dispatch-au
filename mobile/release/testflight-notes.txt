Dispatch Australia 0.1.4 (5)

- New name: Dispatch Australia. Your existing installation and preferences are retained.
- Choose Australia for a combined view of Victoria, ACT, New South Wales, Queensland and South Australia.
- Fixed duplicate Victoria incidents when responsibility changes between emergency services.
- Nearby now makes the selected radius and current/earlier counts clearer.
- Incident times show Updated when only the source update time is known, or Seen when neither a call time nor update is supplied.
- South Australia uses the official CFS incident list. Its feed does not provide coordinates, so choose All South Australia or All Australia without a distance filter to see these records.
- Nearby, Map and Settings remain the only three tabs.

Please test area switching, cross-border incidents, warning details, radius filters and map pins. If your previous Melbourne radius is still selected, choose Australia > All Australia to explore the wider coverage.

Coverage depends on public feeds and does not include every emergency call. NSW and Queensland sources update about every 30 minutes. History begins when each source was connected; this is not a historical backfill. No push notifications or subscriptions are enabled in this preview. In an emergency, call 000.
