Dispatch Victoria — first iOS beta (0.1.0)

Please test:
• Current public incident list and separate official Victoria warnings.
• Category/search filters, Melbourne timestamps and incident details.
• Apple Maps pins and warning areas.
• Selecting a Victorian place and radius, saving/removing one place on this device.
• Light/dark appearance and refreshing after returning to the app.

This is an independent personal preview, not an emergency service. Current data needs an internet connection. Use VicEmergency for official advice and call 000 in an emergency.

Not available in this beta: background push, incident history, cloud account synchronization and paid subscriptions. No purchases are enabled. Saved preferences remain on the device.
